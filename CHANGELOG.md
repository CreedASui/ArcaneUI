# Changelog

## 0.7
- **First release of Arcane UI**, a clean, smooth HUD for Azeroth Survivors, built on Creed UI.
- **Skill bar in three styles:** Classic (Arcane's bronze sigil), Hybrid (a forged medallion with a gem in your class's color, and your class's crest) and Full Class (your class's emblem, crest and colors across the whole UI).
- **The game's notification messages** styled to match and movable; **floating heal and buff text** in the UI's colors.
- Everything else from its Creed UI base: boss timer, boss bars and skulls, damage numbers, damage meter, Stats window, luck medallion, pet bar, loot pop-up, party frames, minimap, nameplates and more, in 11 languages.
